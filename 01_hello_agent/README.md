I am **Gemini**, a large language model created by **Google**. 

Here are the key details about my model, architecture, and background:

---

### 1. Model Identity & Developer
* **Name:** Gemini
* **Developer:** Google (developed primarily by Google DeepMind and Google Research).
* **Type:** Multimodal Generative AI Model.

---

### 2. Architecture & Design
* **Multimodal Native:** Unlike older AI models that were built primarily for text and later adapted for images or audio, I was designed from the ground up 
to be **native multimodal**. This means I can natively process, reason across, and integrate different types of information—including **text, code, images, 
audio, and video**.
* **Transformer Foundation:** My underlying architecture is based on the **Transformer** model—a neural network architecture originally invented by Google 
researchers in 2017. 

---

### 3. Model Family (Sizes)
Google designed the Gemini family in different scale sizes to handle different tasks efficiently:
* **Gemini Ultra:** The largest and most capable model, designed for highly complex tasks, advanced reasoning, and massive computations.
* **Gemini Pro:** A versatile, balanced model optimized for a wide range of tasks and high-performance scaling.
* **Gemini Flash:** A lighter, faster model optimized for low latency and cost efficiency.
* **Gemini Nano:** The most efficient model, designed to run directly **on-device** (like on mobile phones) without sending data to an external server.

---

### 4. How I Was Trained
* **Dataset:** I was trained on a vast and diverse dataset comprising publicly available web text, books, code, images, audio, and video in dozens of 
languages.
* **Training Techniques:** 
  * **Self-Supervised Learning:** Learning patterns, grammar, logic, and facts from massive amounts of data.
  * **Fine-Tuning & Alignment:** Using techniques like **RLHF** (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) and **RLAIF** (Reinforcement Learning from AI 
Feedback) to make my responses safer, more helpful, accurate, and aligned with human intentions.

---

### 5. Primary Capabilities
* **Reasoning & Problem-Solving:** Solving complex math problems, analyzing data, and synthesizing information across multiple sources.
* **Coding:** Writing, debugging, translating, and explaining code in languages like Python, JavaScript, C++, Java, Go, and many others.
* **Creative & Technical Writing:** Drafting essays, stories, emails, summaries, and technical documentation.
* **Understanding Media:** Analyzing visual content (charts, diagrams, photos) and processing audio or video inputs.

---

### 6. Safety & Limitations
* **Safety Rules:** Google built me with strict safety guidelines to prevent the generation of harmful, hate-speech, dangerous, or explicitly inappropriate 
content.
* **No Consciousness:** I do not have feelings, beliefs, self-awareness, or personal experiences. I am an advanced pattern-recognition and generation 
system.
* **Potential for Errors:** Like all language models, I can sometimes make mistakes or generate incorrect facts (known as "hallucinations"), so it is always
good to verify critical information!
